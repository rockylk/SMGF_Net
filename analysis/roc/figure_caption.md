**Fig. 5 | Macro-averaged one-versus-rest ROC performance under mixed and adaptive attacks.**
**a, b,** Mixed attacks at strength 1.0 on CipherSpectrum and USTC-TFC2016, respectively.
**c, d,** Adaptive attacks at strength 1.0 on the corresponding datasets. Each ROC curve is computed by equally averaging the one-versus-rest curves over all 41 CipherSpectrum classes or 12 USTC-TFC2016 classes within each independently trained model. Lines and shaded bands show the mean and one sample standard deviation across three models trained with seeds 42, 43 and 44, evaluated on the same fixed test split (8,200 and 4,252 flows, respectively). Bands describe variability across trained models and are not confidence intervals; displayed bands are bounded by 0 and 1. Legend values report mean AUROC ± sample standard deviation in percent. AUROC is computed from the original class-wise threshold curves within each model and then averaged across models; it is not computed from the interpolated mean display curve. No test samples or training runs are pooled, and model probability vectors are not averaged before calculating AUROC. Dashed diagonal lines indicate chance performance. Source data and analysis code accompany the figure.
