**Supplementary Fig. | Macro-averaged one-versus-rest ROC performance on clean traffic.**
**a, b,** Clean-test ROC curves on CipherSpectrum (41 classes, 8,200 flows) and USTC-TFC2016 (12 classes, 4,252 flows), respectively. Curves are averaged equally across classes within each independently trained model and then across seeds 42, 43 and 44. Lines and shaded bands represent the mean and one sample standard deviation across the three models; bands are bounded by 0 and 1 and are not confidence intervals. Legend AUROC values are calculated from the original per-class threshold curves and report the across-model mean ± sample standard deviation in percent. Three decimal places retain small nonzero standard deviations. Dashed diagonal lines indicate chance performance. Neither samples nor probability vectors are pooled across models. Source data and analysis code accompany the figure.
